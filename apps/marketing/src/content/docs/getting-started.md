Rigel is a desktop app that connects to your Kubernetes clusters, reads them like a senior SRE, and hands you fixes you approve. It runs on your machine and talks to your clusters directly, so there's nothing to deploy or expose.

> **[Download Rigel](/download)** for macOS, Windows, or Linux to get started. It's free while in beta.

## What you'll need

* A Mac, Windows, or Linux machine.
* A Kubernetes cluster and a kubeconfig on your machine, the same one `kubectl` uses.
* Optional: an AI key for the chat copilot. You bring your own; the read and observe panels work without it.

## What it does

The rest of these docs cover what Rigel does once it's running, from the AI chat and the autonomous agent to the app catalog and every resource panel. Have a look around.
